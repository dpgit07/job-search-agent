Generate interview preparation materials. Usage: /prep {company_name}

Steps:
1. Look up the company and role from job_tracker.json
2. Invoke interview-prep subagent
3. Save comprehensive prep package to data/interview_notes/
4. Present a summary of key areas to focus on, including:
   - Top 5 technical topics to review
   - Top 3 system design scenarios
   - Your strongest STAR stories for this company
   - 5 questions to ask the interviewer
